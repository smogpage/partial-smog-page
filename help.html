Getting Help

Users Forum To help facilitate communication between members of the SMOG user community, we have created a listserve where you can post questions. Or, if you are an experienced user, you can help others by posting your insights. Also, when you encounter a problem, you may be able to find the solution in the archive. For more information, see the listserve website.

Contact Us In addition to the users forum, you can email us directly at info@smog-server.org. We always welcome questions about the models, and we try to reply quickly.
